Online Registration for Chess Championships

We at www.OnlineRegistration.CC provide a fully automated, advanced, online player registration platform for chess tournaments with a free pairing & results publishing portal, compatible with multiple payment gateway support, to save your precious time and energy by more than 90% along with keeping accurate online records for all your events.

Streamlined Tournament Management

Create and customize individual or team tournaments with advanced features like automated section filtering, play-up options, and age or rating-specific sections. You can clone past tournaments in one click for quick setups, reducing the time needed to organize new events.

Integrated Player Registration & Payments

Our platform offers a fully automated registration system with support for multiple payment gateways (PayPal, Stripe, and more). You can also offer discounts based on ratings, titles, or early registrations, ensuring an easy and seamless payment process for all players.

         

Seamless Ratings Management

OnlineRegistration.CC automatically updates FIDE/USCF ratings and manages batch files for USCF memberships. You can effortlessly integrate current ratings into pairing files, ensuring that you're always working with up-to-date information for accurate tournament management.
'Process Ratings' function to automatically update the pairing files with the latest player ratings and to generate a list of exceptions with players not eligible for the registered section after the latest ratings update.

Publishing & Real-Time Updates

Broadcast pairings and results instantly, with free publishing of standings and customizable entry lists. Players and spectators will have access to real-time updates, making your events more engaging and easier to manage.

Robust Tools for Organizers

Our platform provides multi-level TD management and bulk player registrations via CSV imports, simplifying the registration process. Plus, the secure environment with 2FA ensures that all data is kept safe and accessible only to authorized personnel.

Chess Membership Database

Our comprehensive Chess Membership Database System streamlines the management of member information, ensuring both accuracy and efficiency. With automated rating calculations and updates, it eliminates manual errors and keeps player ratings up to date in real-time. This system enhances the overall experience for organizers and players alike by providing a seamless, well-organized approach to membership management.
