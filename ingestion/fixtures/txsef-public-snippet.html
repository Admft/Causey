2027 Texas Science and Engineering Fair | April 2–3, 2027

The Texas Science & Engineering Fair (TXSEF) takes place each March in College Station, Texas, at the Texas A&M University Student Recreation Center.

Students must first compete in a Regional Fair and qualify in order to advance to TXSEF.

TXSEF: Texas Science and Engineering Fair

TXSEF brings together 6th-12th grade student finalists from across Texas to showcase their research projects in the fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

The Texas Workforce Commission and ExxonMobil selected Texas A&M Engineering to host the annual competition beginning in 2019.
