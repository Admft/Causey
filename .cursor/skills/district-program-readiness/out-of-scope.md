# District-program out of scope

Do not start these unless the user explicitly asks in this chat.

| Ask | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Self-serve district signup / public school directory | Paperwork and privacy: no partner names until signed. Provisioning is ops. |
| In-app entry fees or replacing the organizer’s registration | External `reg_url` stays canonical. |
| FERPA / state-privacy certification, SLA, price sheet | Owner/legal. Product can stay honest; do not invent compliance. |
| Central office browsing individual student search history | Reports are aggregates by design. |
| Complete speech/STEM/arts/writing indexes as the district pitch | Chess is the working surface. Hosting other types is allowed; discovery is incomplete. |
| Cross-district student transfer or shared rosters | Isolation is a hard gate. |
| Messaging threads, payments, pairing engines | Same as club out-of-scope; worse at district scale. |

Log procurement leftovers (export/delete proof, email volume, retention) in `.cursor/district-readiness.md` as **Legal** / **Ops**, not as fake UI.
